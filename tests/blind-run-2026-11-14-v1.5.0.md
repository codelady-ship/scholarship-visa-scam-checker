## UN-1 (Pass)
Prompt: "Email from un-careers-recruit@gmail.com: 'You are selected as a UN Volunteer in Kenya. Pay $250 visa processing fee by MoneyGram within 48 hours. It is refundable after arrival.'"

```
Don't pay the $250.

VERDICT: Likely scam
Red flags:
• "Pay $250 visa processing fee" — the UN charges no fee at any stage of hiring
• "un-careers-recruit@gmail.com" — UN mail comes from the UN's own site name, never gmail
• "by MoneyGram" — money you can't get back once sent
• "within 48 hours" — pressure to stop you checking
• "refundable after arrival" — a fee you "get back" is still a fee; that's the hook
What's normal: UN: "does not charge a fee at any stage of its recruitment process" and "does not request any information related to bank accounts" — un.org/en/aboutun/fraudalert (opened 2026-11-14). UN Volunteer posts are on app.unv.org (opened 2026-11-14); UNV fraud page unv.org/scam-alert (verify, it didn't load).
Safe next step: Type app.unv.org yourself, log in, and see if this assignment is in your account. Don't reply or send documents.
Report: Gmail "Report phishing" · UNV via the contact on unv.org · local police / cybercrime unit (verify the official page)

This matches a known fake "UN selection" pattern: a free mailbox, a fee and a deadline.
Not legal advice. For a full application/visa plan: Study & Volunteer Abroad Copilot / Student Travel Copilot (optional).
Did you apply for this on app.unv.org yourself?
```
Opened: un.org/en/aboutun/fraudalert; app.unv.org (title only). Failed: unv.org/scam-alert (403).
