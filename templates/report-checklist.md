# Scam Report Checklist

Reporting helps stop the same scam reaching other students, and it creates the record your bank may ask for. Report even if you didn't lose money.

## 1. Collect evidence (before blocking anyone)
- [ ] Screenshots of the full conversation, including the sender's number / profile / e-mail address
- [ ] The website address (copy the full URL; don't open it again)
- [ ] Payment details you were given (account name, IBAN, wallet address, Western Union / MoneyGram reference)
- [ ] Receipts or transfer confirmations, if you paid
- [ ] Dates and times
- [ ] Cover your own passport, ID and card numbers on any copy you share

## 2. Report to the platform
- [ ] WhatsApp / Telegram / Instagram / Facebook: use **Report** on the account or message, then block
- [ ] Housing site or social listing: report the ad and the profile
- [ ] E-mail provider: mark as phishing

## 3. Report to the organisation being impersonated
- [ ] Programme, university or embassy: use the contact or fraud address on its **official** website
- [ ] Visa centre (VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS...): use the "report fraud" option on its official site for your country
- [ ] Your university's international office, if you're already a student there

## 4. Report to the authorities
- [ ] National cybercrime unit or police: [ADD: official reporting page for your country — look it up on a .gov / police site]
- [ ] National consumer-protection body, if one exists: [ADD]
- [ ] If you're abroad: the local police, and tell your own embassy or consulate
- [ ] Examples: US — reportfraud.ftc.gov and ic3.gov; UK — reportfraud.police.uk; Netherlands — police report + platform + bank (verify current pages before use)

## 5. Afterwards
- [ ] Keep the report reference numbers
- [ ] Warn your scholarship / student group (describe the message; don't post anyone's personal data)
- [ ] Be careful with anyone who contacts you offering to "recover" your money for a fee. That is usually a second scam.

*Not legal advice. Reporting routes change; always use the current official page.*
