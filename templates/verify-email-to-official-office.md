# Verification E-mail to an Official Office

Use this to ask the real programme, university, embassy or visa centre whether a message is genuine. **Take the address from the official website**, never from the message you are checking. Attach a screenshot of the message with any personal numbers covered.

Do **not** include your passport number, ID number, passwords or bank details. The office can reply without them.

---

**To:** [ADD: official e-mail from the official website, e.g. the admissions office or programme helpdesk]
**Subject:** Please confirm: is this message from [ADD: programme / university / embassy] genuine?

Dear [ADD: Admissions Office / Programme Team / Visa Section],

I received the message below and want to check that it is genuine before I reply, pay or send any documents.

- **Received on:** [ADD: date]
- **From:** [ADD: sender e-mail / phone number / profile name]
- **Via:** [ADD: e-mail / WhatsApp / Telegram / Instagram / website]
- **What it asks for:** [ADD: e.g. a $150 "processing fee" within 24 hours]

Could you please confirm:

1. Did this message come from your office or an authorised partner?
2. Does your programme charge this fee at this stage?
3. What is the official way to [ADD: pay / submit documents / book an appointment], if any?

A screenshot is attached (personal numbers covered).

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
[ADD: full name]
[ADD: country]
[ADD: application or reference number, only if the official portal gave you one]

---

### Tips
- Until they answer, don't pay and don't reply to the sender.
- If the official site has a "report fraud" form or address, use it as well.
- Keep the original message; don't delete it. It's evidence.
